What it did

Let the software do the nagging

This is one piece of Arlo, the lender portal I designed at EnPowered. A lot of the email tsunami was plain chasing: lenders following up with borrowers for documents, again and again. It cost them time and wore on the relationship. I wanted Arlo to handle the follow-ups while the lender stayed the friendly face. The deal page shows it all at a glance, and a lender can turn reminders off for one deal right there, without touching team settings.

On each deal, the lender sees whether reminders are on, when the next one goes out, who has unsubscribed and which address sends them.

Send from a real person

Lenders connect their own Google or Microsoft account, so invitations and reminders come from someone the borrower has dealt with, not a no-reply address. The screen tells them why: emails from a personal address tend to get better responses. Connecting is one OAuth step, and reconnecting or disconnecting sits behind a single menu.

The empty state: two buttons and one line on why it's worth doing.

Once connected, Reconnect is in plain view and Disconnect is tucked behind the menu.

Lenders keep their own voice

The invitation comes with a sensible default, and lenders can edit the subject and the message in place. Instead of making them paste a URL, I kept it to one rule: text in square brackets becomes the secure upload link. They can write the way they normally write, and the link still works. If they want to send a file along with the invitation, they can attach one.

The subject edits in place, with Cancel and Save right beside it.

A note explains the one rule: square brackets mark the link.

Polite by default

Reminders go out every few days, and only on weekdays between 9 and 5 in the time zone the lender picks. If a borrower unsubscribes, their reminders pause on their own. Nobody gets a reminder about a balance sheet in the middle of the night, and nobody keeps getting emails they asked to stop.

One toggle, one number of days and one time zone. The rules about working hours and unsubscribes are written right under the controls.

What it did

Automatic reminders were part of the Arlo change that cut back-and-forth between lenders and borrowers by 80% and brought document collection down from 2–4 weeks to 1–2 days. I can't separate the reminders' share from that total.
