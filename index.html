I still start with a pencil. Then I direct the work.

I'm a senior product designer with 10+ years in workflow-heavy, rule-bound products: B2B lending platforms, KYC onboarding and mobile banking for 2M+ active customers. These days I spend less time pushing every pixel and more time directing the work: setting the vision and the boundaries, and deciding what's good enough to ship.

From sketch to shipped

01 Think on paper

Me: Ideas start as pencil scribbles. I talk with the client and team, run customer interviews, and plan the MVP with stakeholders.

Agents: Gemini and project agents organize the research and conversations, write up what they found, and help sort my messy thinking.

02 Build in greyscale

Agents: Google Antigravity agents turn the flow into a working greyscale build in hours, not weeks. No colour on purpose, so we judge the flow, not the paint.

Me: I review it, decide what changes, goes or gets added, and feed it back.

03 Put it in front of people

Me: Stakeholders try it, and users too when there's budget for testing. Meanwhile I'm on moodboards, type and colour.

04 Iterate and ship

Figma, with Figma AI, stays the source of truth. Agents build alongside it, and I review every change before it ships.

That's how I took Crewcation from an idea to the App Store as a team of one.

The work you don't see

It can look like vibe coding. Most of the effort lives in the in-between: where data is stored, accounts and security, version control, the design system, and the rules agents work inside so the product stays true to the vision.

What stays human

An agent can tick every UX checkbox and make something usable. It still doesn't know when something shouldn't be built, or whether it feels right. That judgment, on behalf of the person using the product, stays with me.

AI speeds up exploration.

What I'd bring to your team

Untangling complex workflows

I find where people get stuck between tools and teams, design the way through, and measure whether it worked. At EnPowered, loan document collection went from 2–4 weeks to 1–2 days, with 80% less back-and-forth between lenders and borrowers.

Design that respects the rules

At Nedbank I found KYC checks set stricter than the bank's anti-fraud rules required, and redesigned onboarding to cut sign-up drop-off by 25–35% without weakening security.

Systems, not screens

I build and maintain scalable design systems that keep products consistent and speed up engineering delivery, including one system across Crewcation's iOS and web apps.

Growing designers

I've mentored 115+ career-changers at CareerFoundry through 1:1 calls and portfolio and case-study reviews. At least 25 of them now work in design roles.
